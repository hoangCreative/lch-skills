# Vietnamese worked examples

Worked cases for the what-for-and-how engine with the Vietnamese pack loaded. Each one shows a pack signal at work on top of the engine mechanism. Read PACK.md first.

## Example V1, Personal removal, relational terminus (chu hieu)

**Input:** "Toi tranh goi dien cho me 6 thang nay."
PARSE: single subject, the speaker. No tonal-sarcasm flag.
PACK: vietnamese.
SHAPE: REMOVAL. MODE: personal. Pattern signal: 6 thang (longing not met, action repeats).

WHAT FOR (dual track, inner-state rungs spoken as PROPOSAL):
- tranh cuoc goi kho xu -> tranh bi nhac ve ky vong chua dap ung -> (PROPOSAL) doi mat ky vong nghe nhu doi mat viec minh khong la nguoi me muon -> (PROPOSAL) neu lam me that vong, minh dang lam dau nguoi minh thuong.
- L6-falsify: dao them mot lop trả ve "vi con thuong me", mot day. Held.

TERMINUS: relational / chu hieu. "Khong muon la nguyen nhan gay dau cho nguoi minh thuong (me)." NOT forced into an individual-need floor. The Vietnamese pack reading: this is chu hieu plus relational debt, a legitimate floor on its own terms, not "low autonomy".

HOW (bridge to the surface "co nen goi khong"): tranh ne khong giai quyet gi, me da dau san vi su im lang. Serves: the actor.
Step 1 DONE NOW: kich ban cuoc goi dau 5 den 7 phut, mo am, mot chu de an toan, mot cau cho luc me buoc vao vung ky vong ("Da con biet, con dang sap xep, hom nay con chi muon hoi tham me thoi"), va mot cau dong chu dong.

## Example V2, Topic-comment subject-drop (parser signal C3)

**Input:** "Cai du an do bo giua chung roi. Lam tiep de lam gi nua."
PARSE: topic-comment. "Cai du an do bo giua chung" names a topic, no agent stated for who dropped it. Per the pack, this is normal grammar, NOT evasion. Do NOT insert "ai bo" or read motive into the missing actor. The second clause "lam tiep de lam gi nua" is the real handle: a rhetorical-sounding question that is actually a decision-in-doubt.
PACK: vietnamese. SHAPE: STATE leaning toward a REMOVAL decision (stop continuing). MODE: personal.

WHAT FOR: the question is "should I keep going or let it drop". Cascade on the continue/stop decision, not on the phantom subject of "who dropped it". The engine would have wasted a turn hunting a subject the grammar never required. The pack stops that.

(Short case: the lesson is the parse, so this example ends at showing the correct handle rather than a full terminus.)

## Example V3, Modal particle carries the hedge (parser signal C4)

**Input:** "Chac toi nghi viec o cong ty nay qua, co le vay nhe."
PARSE: the terminal "co le vay nhe" is a softening + hedge. The speaker is NOT certain. The pack says: carry this hedge into the terminus; do not write it up as a hard floor.
PACK: vietnamese. SHAPE: ACTION (nghi viec), but held as tentative. MODE: personal.

WHAT FOR: nghi viec -> (tentative) muon thoat cai gi do chua goi ten ro. Because the input is hedged, the right move is NOT to declare a confident terminus. It is to surface ONE specific question rather than commit on thin, softened ground:
> "Khi anh noi 'co le vay nhe', nghe nhu anh chua chac. Cai khien anh muon di, va cai khien anh con ngan ngai, cu the la gi?"

TERMINUS: deliberately NOT declared. The particle told us the ground is too soft. This is the pack protecting the engine from over-certifying a floor.

## Example V4, Thoi-ke read (speech-act, C6)

**Input:** "Thoi ke di, toi khong tranh nua dau."
PARSE: "thoi ke" is an act of releasing / acceptance, not a decision-to-act and not avoidance. Running the ACTION cascade ("buong de dat duoc gi?") would force a goal onto an act of letting-go.
PACK: vietnamese. SHAPE: read as STATE / relational acceptance, not ACTION. MODE: personal.

WHAT FOR: the engine does NOT drill "thoi ke" as if it were a plan with a hidden objective. Instead it reads the release: what is being put down, and is the release itself the relief sought. If anything is excavated, it is the tension that preceded the letting-go, not the letting-go as a means to an end.
TERMINUS (if reached, PROPOSAL): often "muon thoi cang thang / khong con phai gong", an acceptance floor, not an achievement floor.

The point: the pack prevents the engine from misclassifying a Vietnamese release-act as a goal-directed action.

## Example V5, Regional face, Hue restraint vs Northern display (terminus C1)

**Input:** "Toi nguoi Hue. Dot nay met lam nhung toi van noi voi moi nguoi la on."
PARSE: no surface crisis tokens, calm affect. PACK clinical-display note fires: Hue norm masks distress behind composure. Do NOT under-weight the calm. SHAPE: STATE. MODE: personal.

WHAT FOR (careful, container-checked): the calm is not the absence of weight. Cascade gently. If a face terminus is reached, the pack says pick the REGIONAL reading: for Hue, the floor is more likely "giu the dien bang su kin tieng, khong muon lam phien nguoi khac" (composure and not-burdening), NOT the Northern "giu the dien truoc dam dong" (public display). Misreading it as the Northern variant would name the wrong floor.

HOW: serves the actor; gentle, do not perform alarm (which itself burdens a Hue speaker). Step 1: a quiet, specific check-in that does not force public emotional display.

Note: if the same words came with self-harm signals, the clinical brake overrides everything here.

## Example V6, Observed system, structural (engine, VN surface)

**Input:** "Google tra loi thang tren trang chu, khong dan link ra ngoai. De lam gi?"
PARSE: subject is a system, clean. SHAPE: PHENOMENON. MODE: structural. (No Vietnamese pack terminus needed here; the floor is a brute structural fact, which is engine-level. Shown to demonstrate the engine runs unchanged under the pack.)

WHAT FOR (NODE + LINK, grade named): tra loi tai cho -> giu phien (NODE: hanh vi zero-click do duoc, bang chung do-luong, hang cao; LINK: khong co click ra ngoai thi giu duoc chu y tren nen tang) -> nhieu be mat quang cao moi phien (NODE: mo hinh quang cao cong khai; LINK: chu y duoc kiem tien qua quang cao) -> vi the nen tang manh hon.
TERMINUS: brute structural fact (rang buoc giu-chu-y-thanh-doanh-thu). Constraint vs tendency: day la rang buoc dong luc, khong phai "Google muon". No personification, no invented numbers.

HOW (to the observer): cai gi can theo doi va cach dinh vi neu ban phu thuoc luong truy cap ra ngoai. A strategic read, not a prescription for Google.
